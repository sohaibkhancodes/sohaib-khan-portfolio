Games

Online game: Chess

Chess is a two-player strategy game that can be played online on websites such as Lichess and Chess.com. Each player controls sixteen pieces and tries to checkmate the opponent’s king. It rewards planning ahead, patience and careful decisions.

Physical game: Cricket

Cricket is a team sport played with a bat and ball on an outdoor field, with two teams of eleven players. One team bats to score runs while the other bowls and fields. It is very popular in Pakistan and builds teamwork and fitness.
